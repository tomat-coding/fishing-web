When to Fish Japan's Lakes, Month by Month

What's in season on each of the six lakes, from winter dome boats to spring trophy bass, so you can pick the month first and the lake second.

Updated October 18, 2026

Each of the six lakes we cover has its own season, and they barely overlap. Spring is for trophy bass on Biwa and Kasumigaura, autumn for numbers of bass and Inawashiro's heaviest smallmouth, and winter belongs to wakasagi, from Suwa's heated dome boats and, when the ice allows, through the ice at Ogawara. This calendar shows what's worth planning around in each month. The details, and the rules, are in each lake's own guide.

The year at a glance

  • Trophy bass: spring, March to May, on Biwa and Kasumigaura. May is the month most Biwa guides point to.
  • Smallmouth bass: Inawashiro, May to November, with the heaviest fish in autumn.
  • Numbers of bass: autumn, September to November, on Biwa and Kasumigaura.
  • Wakasagi from a heated dome boat: Suwa, roughly late September to March, busiest from December to mid-February.
  • Ice fishing for wakasagi: Ogawara (Anenuma), roughly mid-January to early March, only when the ice is safe.
  • Salmon: Saroma, pink salmon from summer and chum salmon into autumn.
  • Hand-gathering shijimi clams: Ogawara, July 1 to August 31 (2026 dates).
Dates move every year

Ice seasons, dome-boat opening days and gathering areas are set fresh each season, and warm winters have cancelled ice fishing outright. Use this calendar to pick a month, then check the lake's own guide and the official notices it links to before you book.

January and February

The coldest months. Bass slow right down, and the action moves to wakasagi, on heated boats and on the ice where it holds.

Suwa · dome boats at their busiest

Peak season for Suwa's heated dome boats, so book ahead for weekends. Some areas close to protect spawning fish from January 1.

Ogawara · ice fishing, if the ice holds

Anenuma's managed ice-fishing ground usually opens from mid-January, but only while the ice is safe. In 2026 the cooperative cancelled ice fishing on Uchinuma altogether, so check Ogawara's notices first.

Inawashiro · ice on Lake Akimoto

Inawashiro itself doesn't freeze, but the same cooperative runs ice fishing on nearby Lake Akimoto when it can. In 2026 that window lasted barely a week in February.

Biwa · slow, deep bass

Bass on Biwa hold in deep water and around structure, especially in the north basin. Bites are few, but a quiet winter day can still turn up a very big fish. Biwa trout are open, by approved boat or charter.

Saroma · chika through the ice

In cold winters, parts of Saroma freeze and people fish for chika through the ice. Ask locally before walking out.

Kasumigaura · quiet

Slow for bass on Kasumigaura, and wakasagi are closed from January 21 to February 28.

March and April

Spring arrives from the south. The wind can be the biggest obstacle, especially on Biwa.

Biwa and Kasumigaura · pre-spawn bass

The biggest bass start moving in March, and spawning gets going from mid to late April. Biwa's shallow south basin warms first, and the north basin runs a few weeks behind. Early spring brings the strongest hira-oroshi winds on Biwa.

Saroma · flounder

Flounder fishing on Saroma starts around March and builds through April.

Inawashiro · stream trout open

Iwana and yamame open on April 1 at Inawashiro.

Biwa · ko-ayu begin

Families start fishing for ko-ayu from Biwa's harbors and river mouths from around April.

Suwa and Ogawara · winter winds down

Suwa's dome boats run into March, and Ogawara's ice season ends by early March at the latest.

May and June

Arguably the best all-round months, if you can avoid the Golden Week crowds in early May.

Biwa · the trophy month

May is the month most Biwa guides point to for a big bass. Ko-ayu peak from May to July.

Inawashiro · spring smallmouth

Heavy pre-spawn smallmouth move onto shallow sandy flats around 2 m deep at Inawashiro. The Japan-record fish came in late May.

Saroma · peak flounder

Flounder peak from April to June, and the ports get busy around Golden Week.

Kasumigaura · post-spawn

Bass recover after spawning, and one local guide rates mid-June to mid-July as the most fun stretch of the year on Kasumigaura. Carp are closed from May 11 to June 10 and wakasagi from May 1 to July 20.

July and August

Hot, humid and best early or late in the day.

Ogawara · shijimi gathering

Ogawara opens its shijimi area from July 1 to August 31 (closed August 13–16 in 2026). Hands only.

Saroma · pink salmon

Pink salmon arrive at Saroma from summer. Early morning is the classic window, and some river mouths are closed, so check the notices.

Biwa · summer weed

Thick weed in Biwa's south basin makes it a game of edges and holes. Ayu are protected from August 21, with rod fishing allowed until August 31.

Kasumigaura · mornings and evenings

Little shade on Kasumigaura, so fish the low-light hours and moving water near the sluice gates. The traditional sail-trawling boats sail for visitors from late July.

September and October

Autumn feeding starts, and the wakasagi season opens.

Biwa and Kasumigaura · numbers of bass

Bass feed up before winter and are easier to catch. Local anglers on Kasumigaura often single out September. Biwa trout close from October 1 to November 30.

Inawashiro · heaviest smallmouth

Autumn smallmouth at Inawashiro are at their heaviest, fished deep through the day and in the shallows at dawn and dusk. Iwana and yamame close on September 30, and wakasagi run from October.

Suwa · shore fishing, then dome boats

Shore fishing for small wakasagi is at its best in early autumn, and Suwa's dome boats start between late September and October.

Saroma · chum salmon

Chum salmon follow the pink salmon into autumn at Saroma.

November and December

The switch from autumn to winter.

Biwa · into winter

Autumn patterns give way to winter ones on Biwa. Biwa trout reopen in December for approved boats and charters.

Suwa · main dome-boat season

Dome boats on Suwa are in full swing, and weekends fill up from December.

Inawashiro · last of the smallmouth

The smallmouth season at Inawashiro runs to the end of November.

Saroma · winter fishing begins

Smelt and cod season starts on Saroma in December.

Ogawara · watch for the ice announcement

Nothing to fish on the ice yet. Keep an eye on Ogawara's notices for the coming season.

Sources

Every season and date here comes from our lake guides, which link the official sources behind them: the fisheries cooperatives, prefectural rules and the operators themselves. Closed seasons are those in force for 2026, and the Suwa opening day is for the 2026–27 season. Check the guide for your lake, and its sources, before you book.

Pick the month, then the lake, then check that lake's notices the week you go. Seasons here move with the weather, not the calendar.
